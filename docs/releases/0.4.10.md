**中文**

- **改进** 统计快捷范围调整为本周、上周、本月、上月、今年与自定义，默认使用本周。
- **修复** 本周每日聆听固定展示周一至周日完整七天，未来日期保留为零值柱。

**English**

- **Improved** Listening statistics shortcuts now offer this week, last week, this month, last month, this year, and a custom range, with this week selected by default.
- **Fixed** The daily listening chart for this week now always shows all seven days from Monday through Sunday, leaving future days at zero.
