**中文**

- **新增** 输出设备与麦克风支持右键隐藏；隐藏状态按设备 UID 长期保存，可在“已隐藏设备”中恢复。正在使用或属于多设备输出的设备不可隐藏。
- **修复** 启动或退出时自动清理上次遗留的 Auxmo 多设备聚合，避免重新打开应用后残留组合阻止重新设置。
- **改进** 隐藏设备列表与普通设备行对齐，显示数量、悬停反馈和箭头旋转动画；多设备输出箭头默认向右。

**English**

- **Added** Output devices and microphones can be hidden from their context menus. Hidden devices persist by UID and can be restored from Hidden Devices. Devices in use or selected for multi-device output cannot be hidden.
- **Fixed** Auxmo now removes orphaned multi-device aggregates left by a previous session at launch or quit, so reopening the app no longer leaves a stale group blocking a new setup.
- **Improved** Hidden-device lists now align with device rows, show counts, hover feedback, and animated chevrons; the multi-device output chevron now points right by default.
