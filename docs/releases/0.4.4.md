**中文**

- **新增** 点击设备或应用排行可查看详情，包括累计时长、每日趋势、使用时段和关联应用或设备。
- **新增** 设置中的统计数据管理：查看首条记录日期、导出 JSON、确认后清除本地记录。
- **改进** 新记录按应用已确认的输出路由统计设备关系，避免不同应用的设备被交叉归属；路由未确认时不猜测输出设备。旧记录仍是估算。
- **改进** 补充统计详情的导航、悬停反馈及中英文界面。

**English**

- **Added** Device and app statistics details with listening totals, daily trends, listening hours, and related apps or output devices.
- **Added** Statistics management in Settings: view the first recorded date, export JSON, and clear local records after confirmation.
- **Improved** New records associate each app with its confirmed output route rather than assigning every app to every output; unconfirmed routes are not guessed. Historical associations remain estimates.
- **Improved** Navigation, hover feedback, and Chinese and English presentation in statistics details.
