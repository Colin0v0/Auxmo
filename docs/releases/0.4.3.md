**中文**

- **新增** 完整详情窗口，使用侧边栏查看输出、麦克风、耳机控制、聆听统计和设置；菜单栏轻量面板继续保留。
- **新增** 本机聆听统计，查看每日趋势、设备和应用使用情况、使用时段与会话估算。统计基于 Mac 上观察到的音频活动，历史数据与会话时长为估算。
- **新增** 耳机低电量提醒：在设置中授权并开启后，耳机电量达到 20% 或以下时通过系统通知提醒。
- **改进** 完整窗口与统计页面支持中英文，修复麦克风页面未及时读取设备的问题。

**English**

- **Added** A full detail window with sidebar navigation for output, microphones, headphone controls, listening statistics, and settings, alongside the compact menu-bar panel.
- **Added** Local listening statistics for daily trends, devices, apps, listening hours, and estimated sessions. Statistics are based on audio activity observed on the Mac; historical data and session durations are estimates.
- **Added** Optional low-battery notifications when a connected headphone reports 20% or less, after enabling the feature and granting notification permission.
- **Improved** Chinese and English coverage for the full window and statistics, and fixed microphone devices not loading promptly on the microphone page.
